# Audience and go-to-market: diaspora, calendar, AI rules, pricing

Desk research, 4 Oct 2026. Census, Pew, ONS and StatCan pages were blocked by egress policy, so figures come from search-result text. **Unverified** means one weak or conflicting source.

## Summary

- **Languages.** Diaspora numbers justify Hindi, Telugu and Tamil, but not Kannada, the smallest pilot language in every market. Gujarati has a stronger case: most British Hindus and the BAPS network are Gujarati. Punjabi is large but mostly Sikh; Bengali is large but mostly Bangladeshi.
- **Channels.** Chinmaya has 18,000 Bala Vihar children and BAPS 116 mandirs in North America. ISKCON group bookings carried Mahavatar Narsimha.
- **Calendar.** *Ramayana: Rise of a Legend* opens worldwide on 6 Nov 2026, two days before Diwali (8 Nov). Part 2 is due at Diwali 2027 (29 Oct). Mahavatar Parshuram is due in Dec 2027.
- **Rules.** India's synthetic-media rules have been in force since 20 Feb 2026. They require a prominent visible label, a spoken disclosure before AI audio, and permanent metadata. A draft from 30 Mar 2026 would make the label continuous.
- **Price.** $39/yr matches Hoichoi ($39.99) and undercuts JioHotstar UK (£69.99). ₹149/mo is now above Spotify India (₹139).

## 4. AI-content rules

| Rule | Status | Requires |
|---|---|---|
| IT (Intermediary) Amendment Rules 2026, G.S.R. 120(E) | Notified 10 Feb 2026, in force 20 Feb 2026 | Visual synthetic content gets a "prominent" visible label; the October 2025 draft's 10%-of-frame size was dropped. Audio gets a disclosure played before it. Permanent metadata carries a unique identifier and the generating resource. Nobody may strip labels or metadata. Large platforms (SSMIs) must collect a user declaration and verify it. Content must come down within 3 hours of a lawful order |
| Draft amendment, 30 Mar 2026 | Comments closed 7 May 2026. Not confirmed as notified (**unverified** as of Oct 2026) | The label must stay "continuous and clearly visible… throughout the duration" |
| IT Rules 2021, Part III (OTT Code of Ethics) | In force | If we count as a publisher of online curated content: an age rating and content descriptor at the start of every programme, three-tier grievance redress, and due caution over religious groups |
| YouTube | Disclosure since 2024; "inauthentic" monetisation rule from 15 Jul 2025 | Realistic synthetic content must be disclosed; animated or unrealistic content is exempt. Mass-produced repetitive content is demonetised |
| Meta | 2024 | Reads C2PA and IPTC metadata and labels posts "AI info" automatically |
| Apple guideline 1.1.5 | Current | Bars "inaccurate or misleading quotations of religious texts" |
| Google Play AI-generated content policy | 2024 | Only for apps that generate content; requires in-app reporting |

## Implications for Dheevara

**Pilot language order.**
1. English and Hindi carry the base.
2. Then Telugu: about 606k US speakers, the second home language of Indian immigrants (15%).
3. Then Tamil, which is present in all four markets.
4. Then Gujarati, ahead of Kannada, for 492k US speakers, the core of British Hinduism and the BAPS network.
5. Move Kannada to the first post-pilot wave unless a Kannada lineage partner or reviewer makes it cheap. Malayalam joins that wave for the UAE.
6. Tie Bengali to a Devī arc for Durga Puja 2027 (Mahalaya around 29 Sep 2027).
7. Punjabi comes last, and only with content that does not depict the Sikh Gurus.

If accepted, record this in `docs/decisions.md`.

**Launch window.**
- Skip Navaratri (11 Oct): no three weeks' notice.
- Target 4–8 Nov 2026 with free content: the Mainaka JS experience, Shorts and Reels on a Sundara Kāṇḍa episode, and an audio chapter, all live for *Ramayana*'s 6 Nov opening.
- Open the paid cohorts at Ugadi, Rāma Navamī and Hanumān Jayantī (7–20 Apr 2027). The Hanumān arc fits, and Ugadi suits the Telugu launch.
- Time the season-one finale to Navaratri and Diwali 2027 and *Ramayana* Part 2 (29 Oct 2027). Prepare a Paraśurāma chapter for December 2027.

**Channel order.**
1. Shorts, Reels and WhatsApp.
2. Chinmaya Bala Vihar: English, a 30-week syllabus and 18k children; offer chapters as teaching aids.
3. BAPS for Gujarati, and ISKCON for group viewing on the Mahavatar Narsimha model.
4. HSS and temples.

Announce every festival drop three weeks ahead through these networks.

**Pricing checks.**
- $39 a year sits at the regional-OTT anchor (Hoichoi $39.99), well below Audible or Spotify.
- Test a $49 family plan, and quarterly plans in the UK and Canada, matching JioHotstar.
- In India, ₹149 a month is above Spotify (₹139) and below Mythik+'s ₹3,499 a year. Test ₹99 a month against ₹999 a year.
- UAE pricing is not set in the plan.

**Compliance for the player.**
- Put a visible AI label on every frame, continuous behind the planned flag, and burn it into shared cuts, since re-encoding strips metadata.
- Play a spoken AI disclosure before Bulbul narration and Vāgdhenu chant, including in screen-off mode. A human reciter's recording needs none.
- Embed a C2PA manifest with a unique id in every MP4 and audio file. The ffmpeg assembler must preserve it.
- Declare synthetic content on every YouTube and Meta upload.
- Show an age rating (U) and a content descriptor at the start of each chapter, and name a grievance officer. A lawyer must confirm whether the Part III Code of Ethics applies to us.
- Keep a contact who can answer a takedown order within 3 hours.
- The source verse beside every quotation is also our defence under Apple guideline 1.1.5.

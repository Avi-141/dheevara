# Competitors: AI mythology, audio drama, devotional and diaspora apps

Desk research, 4 Oct 2026, from search results (most pages were egress-blocked). Each fact carries its source date, or "accessed Oct 2026". **Unverified** means no source confirmed it.

## Summary

Mythik is closer to our pitch than the plan assumes. It hires Sanskrit research fellows under Oxford and Harvard scholar advisers, and sells Mythik+ in India at ₹3,499 a year, 3.5 times our ₹999, in English and Hindi only. Our edge is visible per-claim citation and language, not "having scholars". Pocket FM proves the audio habit (155 minutes a day, $500M run rate, ~70% US) and now sells retention-aware AI writing (Sherpa). Its coins draw "$1,000 to finish a story" complaints, and it shut its video app after five months. Kuku is industrialising AI video at under $100 a minute, and shows how brand adjacency and ₹2-trial autopay traps hurt a devotional brand. JioHotstar's AI Mahabharat shows views and trust come apart: 26.5M views, IMDb 1.4. Sri Mandir confirms roughly 10x diaspora ARPU. Mahavatar Narsimha shows temple networks can carry a stylised multilingual release. Kids' apps already use the śloka card. Sourced, cinematic, multilingual chapters remain unclaimed.

## Mythik / Mythik+

- **Company.** Founded April 2025 in Mumbai by Jason Kothari, formerly of Freecharge, Housing.com and Snapdeal. It raised a $15M seed with Sakal Media Group and Shah Rukh Khan's family office among the backers ([Sakal, May 2025](https://sakalmediagroup.com/mythik-an-ai-based-entertainment-startup-raises-15m-to-build-the-disney-from-the-east-ap-globale-invests-via-sakal-media-group)). It then raised $5M "at over $50M" from Harsh Jain, Blume Founders Fund, Bharti Mittal's family office and others ([inc42, 20 May 2026](https://inc42.com/buzz/ai-entertainment-startup-mythik-raises-5-mn-at-50-mn-valuation/)). Dealroom lists the seed at a $60M valuation, which would make the 2026 round flat or down (**unverified**) ([Dealroom, accessed Oct 2026](https://dealroom.co/companies/mythik/)).
- **Product.** 10–12 minute episodes (inc42, above). Mythik+ on iOS, released 6 Aug 2026, offers "premium series… audiobooks & mantras" and "interconnected character universes" in English and Hindi, "researched by experts". It ranks outside the top 30 in US free Entertainment ([store listing via mwm.ai, accessed Oct 2026](https://mwm.ai/apps/mythik-transcend-time/6752926640)). Android availability is **unverified**.
- **Price.** ₹3,499 for one year, ₹5,999 for two and ₹7,999 for three, each shown against higher struck-through prices ([mythik.com in search results, accessed Oct 2026](https://www.mythik.com/)). The USD price is **unverified**.
- **Sourcing.** A February 2026 job post for a "Research Fellow in Sanskrit & Indian Mythology" asks for fluency in Sanskrit and the epic and Purāṇic corpus. The fellow works with "a global advisory network of scholars, led by Prof. Diwakar Acharya and Prof. Parimal Patil" ([INDOLOGY list, Feb 2026](https://list.indology.info/pipermail/indology/2026-February/061736.html)). I found no on-screen citation.
- **Reception.** No app-store review themes or public criticism found (**unverified**). The plan's 3.7M members, 1B+ views and 750+ creatives are also **unverified**.
- **For Dheevara.** *Gap:* per-claim citation with the page one tap away, southern languages, and short chapters. *Correct the plan:* "Retellings, no visible sourcing" should read "scholar-advised, but no visible citation".

## Pocket FM

- **Scale.** A $500M run rate, up 70% year on year, with the US at about 70%. Listeners average 155 minutes a day and 51% of weekly actives listen daily ([YourStory, Sep 2026](https://yourstory.com/2026/09/pocket-entertainment-crosses-500m-in-arr-fuelled-by-ai); [Pulse 2.0, Sep 2026](https://pulse2.com/pocket-entertainment-surpasses-500-million-arr-as-ai-entertainment-platform-grows-70-year-over-year/)). AI makes 93% of the catalogue and 99% of new content ([AI Weekly, Sep 2026](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/pocket-fm-doubles-arr-to-500m-as-ai-runs-99-of-new-audio)). The plan's "135 min/day for US listeners" is **unverified**.
- **Coins.** 30 free minutes a day, then episodes cost from pennies to over $3. "My Vampire System" runs 4,000+ cliffhanger episodes and grossed about $90M ([Forbes, Sep 2026](https://www.forbes.com.au/news/uncategorized/more-content-is-always-great-inside-pocket-fms-400-million-content-farm/)). Reviewers estimate $1,000+ to finish a story, and a "Finale Zone" locks endings ([Unstar, 2026](https://unstar.app/blog/is-pocket-fm-legit-worth-it-audio-series-app-reviews-2026)). The app scores 2.5 on Trustpilot against 4.7 in the stores ([Trustpilot, accessed Oct 2026](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/pocketfm.com)).
- **Pocket TV.** The microdrama app was shut on 25 June 2026 after about five months in beta; staff were moved back to audio ([exchange4media, Jun 2026](https://www.exchange4media.com/digital-news/pocket-fm-to-shut-its-microdrama-app-pocket-tv-155760.html); [NewsBytes, Jun 2026](https://www.newsbytesapp.com/news/business/pocket-fm-says-pocket-tv-shutdown-is-part-of-strategy/story)).
- **Sherpa.** Launched 19 Sep 2026 as a free AI fiction-writing tool built on "Narrative Intelligence". Separate agents handle ideation, structure, research, prose and review, tracking characters and plotlines across hundreds of episodes, and it "place[s] moments where listeners are most likely to keep going". Pocket FM credits it with a 75x rise in output, and markets it to creators with a satirical launch film ([Digital Terminal, Sep 2026](https://digitalterminal.in/infotainment/pocket-fm-launches-sherpa-ai-to-transform-fiction-writing-and-story-creation); [MediaNews4U, 23 Sep 2026](https://www.medianews4u.com/pocket-fm-launches-sherpa-with-campaign-across-india-and-the-us/)).
- **Mythology.** A 60-episode Hindi adaptation of Kevin Missal's *Kalki Trilogy* ([Filmibeat, 2026](https://filmibeat.com/bollywood/viral/2026/pocket-fm-announces-kevin-missal-s-the-kalki-trilogy-strengthening-its-ip-led-audio-slate-for-2026-497212.html)).
- **For Dheevara.** *Copy:* a daily-habit cadence and narrative memory (our canon graph). *Avoid:* coin walls and locked finales. *Lesson:* video economics failed even for the market leader.

## Implications for Dheevara

1. **Rewrite the Authority axis.** Mythik has named scholar advisers, so our claim must be *visible, checkable citation per line plus the scanned page*. Update the product-plan table.
2. **Revisit India pricing.** ₹999 a year is 29% of Mythik's ₹3,499, below ACK's ₹2,199 and level with Kuku's ~₹899. Test ₹1,499–1,999 for a trust product. Get Mythik's USD price before fixing $39.
3. **Lead with southern languages.** Mythik is English and Hindi only. Tamil, Telugu and Kannada at the pilot is a real gap against Mythik, though not against Kuku or Pocket FM.
4. **Market "no coins, no trick trials".** Explicit renewal reminders and one-tap cancel, against the Pocket FM and Kuku complaints.
5. **Borrow Pocket FM's retention craft, not its volume.** Sherpa's "moments where listeners keep going" is our retention beat. A daily audio cadence may matter more than weekly video.
6. **Put placement terms in every licensing deal.** Never sit beside erotic or non-devotional content (Kuku's Bhakti/LoveNasha lesson).
7. **Treat faces, lip-sync and props as the named failure modes.** Ship the anachronism gate and no-close-up rule before the pilot. Correct the plan: 6.5M views was day one, not week one.
8. **The ~10x diaspora ARPU holds** (Sri Mandir, ₹7,000 vs ₹600–800). Pursue Sri Mandir or temple apps for festival-bundle distribution.
9. **Build an age-gated Bal Vihar pack.** Chapter, reveal, quiz and lineage card. Gurukula and Lipi validate the format; none of them cite.
10. **Run the Mahavatar playbook.** Simultaneous multilingual release, a temple group-screening kit, and a sourced Paraśurāma arc timed to *Mahavatar Parshuram*.
11. **Choose the look away from ACK** unless ACK becomes a licensing partner.

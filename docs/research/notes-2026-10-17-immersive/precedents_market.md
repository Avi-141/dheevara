# Precedents and market for immersive, interactive cultural and mythological experiences (as of 17 Oct 2026)

Scope: immersive, location-based, glasses, AR/VR and hologram precedents relevant to Dheevara. These notes extend, and do not repeat, `docs/research/notes-2026-10-15-api-first/positioning_landscape.md` and `docs/research/product/{competitors,audience-gtm,synthesis}.md`. Items already covered there (Mythik, JioHotstar AI Mahabharat, the Rinku Singh and Organiser FIR cases, Mahavatar Narsimha and ISKCON group bookings, ACK's "immersive experiences" CEO remark, the AI-label rules) are marked "(repo)" and not re-sourced.

Method: desk research from web search on 17 Oct 2026, with full-page fetches where possible. Many figures come from a search snippet or one press report and are marked as such. Vendor case studies (Christie, Proto) are promotional. Government footfall figures are estimates and often conflict.

## 1. Indian mythology and heritage immersive work, 2023–2026

### Takeaway
India already has a large state- and temple-funded immersive layer: projection-mapping and laser shows, VR-darshan counters, hologram museums and Kumbh experience centres. It is paid for by tenders and capex (about ₹5–20 cr per show, up to ₹100–240 cr per museum), and tickets cost about ₹50–100. Almost all of it is spectacle or darshan. None of it is sourced, and much of it shows deities directly (holograms of Ram Darbar, Shiva, VR darshan of the mūrti). The cell "sourced witness retelling" is empty here too. The money is in B2B content and production contracts, not in tickets.

### Cited findings

**Ayodhya (Ram temple ecosystem)**
- **Ram Katha Museum, Ayodhya.** Redeveloped for the Ram Janmabhoomi Teertha Kshetra trust by **Tagbin** (Gurgaon). Tagbin's CEO expected interior costs **under ₹100 cr (~$11.5M)**. It has three floors, 40,000 sq ft and 15+ galleries, using AI, holograms and interactive 3D. **A hologram of Ram Darbar is planned.** Opening was due "next year" (i.e. 2026); I could not confirm it has opened ([The Art Newspaper, 22 Jan 2025](https://www.theartnewspaper.com/2025/01/22/museum-india-grand-ram-temple-ayodhya)).
  - Criticism in the same piece: curator Ram Rahman says it will tell "a Hindutva version of the Ram story" and that "Spectacle doesn't replace learning and proper research". Tagbin's CEO said the museum will omit the movement's controversies and pointed to Tagbin's Prime Ministers' Museum as a precedent (same source).
  - The temple itself cost about $215M and was inaugurated 22 Jan 2024 (same source).
- **Deepotsav shows.** A 45-minute projection-mapping show at Ram Ki Paidi narrates Ram's return, alongside a laser and projection "Ram Katha" show. No ticket price was found; the shows appear to be free public festival programming ([Organiser, Sep 2025](https://organiser.org/2025/09/21/316970/bharat/deepotsav-2025-ayodhya-prepares-for-record-breaking-deepotsav-26-lakh-diyas-to-illuminate-saryu-ghats/); [Outlook](https://www.outlookindia.com/national/ayodhya-deepotsav-around-18-lakh-diyas-to-be-lit-laser-show-ramlilas-part-of-celebrations-news-231974)).
- **Ramayana wax museum** (Sunil's Wax Museum). Opened at Deepotsav, Oct 2025: 50+ life-size wax figures from Ram's childhood to the coronation, 3D light effects and soundscapes. Tickets **₹100**; 100 visitors at a time ([Storyboard18, 21 Oct 2025](https://www.storyboard18.com/amp/how-it-works/ayodhya-opens-worlds-first-ramayana-wax-museum-ahead-of-diwali-82872.htm)).
- **Footfall** (the demand pool any Ayodhya experience sits in). The UP government said about **23 crore visitors in Jan–Jun 2025**. Full-year 2025 estimates of 45–50 cr are projections, not counts. 2024 is given as 16.44 cr. Figures conflict across sources ([Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india%2Futtar-pradesh%2Fover-55-crore-devotees-visited-ayodhyas-ram-temple-since-consecration-up-govt-3606402); [ETV Bharat, Jun 2025](https://www.etvbharat.com/en/!state/ram-temple-in-ayodhya-receives-over-five-crore-visitors-since-inauguration-enn25062802809); [Swarajya](https://swarajyamag.com/amp/story/news-brief/two-years-after-pran-pratishtha-ram-temple-fuels-ayodhyas-religious-tourism-led-economic-boom)).

**Kurukshetra (Mahābhārata)**
- **Jyotisar projection-mapping show.** Inaugurated Nov 2022 by Om Birla and CM Khattar: a 24-minute show on the 40-ft Virāṭ Svarūpa statue of Krishna, using a 30,000-lumen projector, lasers and fire effects. Current operating status and ticketing are unverified ([Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/haryana/projection-mapping-show-at-jyotisar-inaugurated-452690); [Haryana CM office](https://mail.manoharlalkhattar.in/node/28887)).
- **Jyotisar Anubhav Kendra.** A Mahābhārata museum and interpretation centre with a state budget of **₹205 cr** (one explainer says ₹240 cr). Modi inaugurated it virtually on ~13 Feb 2024, with only **2 of 5 galleries** open. A later Tribune report says it was still delayed and incomplete ([Travel Trade Journal](https://traveltradejournal.com/jyotisar-anubhav-kendra-to-be-built-at-kurukshetra-at-rs-205-crore-chief-minister-khattar/); [Drishti IAS](https://www.drishtiias.com/state-pcs-current-affairs/jyotisar-anubhav-kendra/print_manually); [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/haryana/cm-reviews-jyotisar-project-urges-fast-track-completion/amp)).

**Ujjain and Kashi**
- **Mahakal Lok light and laser show.** A 25-minute show by the temple committee, made paid at **₹100 per person** in July 2026 after seven months free ([Patrika, 15 Jul 2026, Hindi](https://www.patrika.com/ujjain-news/devotees-will-now-have-to-pay-for-the-ujjain-mahakal-temple-light-and-sound-show-20667908)). The vendor already supplied Ujjain and Vaishno Devi ([Hinduism Today, 18 Jun 2024](https://www.hinduismtoday.com/hpi/2024/06/18/this-temple-introduces-virtual-reality-darshan-for-devotees-amid-heatwave/)). The CEO said **363 employees and priests** watched it between 5 May and 4 Jun 2024 and gave positive feedback ([DT Next](https://www.dtnext.in/news/national/up-kashi-vishwanath-temple-introduces-virtual-reality-darshan-for-devotees-790298)).
- **Durlabh Darshan** (founder Prashant Mishra, also TechXR).
  - Footprint: "6D" VR centres (headsets plus air, water and fragrance effects) in seven states: Kashi, Ujjain, Vaishno Devi, Ayodhya, Haridwar, Nagpur and Delhi. It claims "lakhs" of users and a filed patent.
  - New content from 1 Jul 2026: "Shri Ram Ki Ayodhya" and "Ujjaini Ke Mahakaal", narrated by actor **Ashutosh Rana**, written by Aalok Shrivastav. Mobile VR kits cover 60+ sites.
  - Plans: Char Dham, the Jyotirlingas, the Shakti Peeths and Kailash, plus "Shubh Darshan" AR smart frames and idols for home ritual.
  - No prices or revenue disclosed ([Daily Pioneer, 2 Jul 2026](https://dailypioneer.com/news/durlabh-darshan-plans-6d-virtual-spiritual-experience-centres)).
- **Tirumala (TTD)** is preparing VR headsets for devotees in the queue lines, showing Tirumala and "sacred visuals related to Lord Srivaru". It is donation-funded and at an early stage as of Apr 2026 ([ETV Bharat, Apr 2026](https://www.etvbharat.com/en/state/ttd-to-introduce-vr-experience-in-tirumala-queue-lines-enn26040804042)).
- A VR Bhasma Āratī counter at Mahakal Lok is listed by a booking site (promotional; unverified) ([ujjaindarshan.com](https://www.ujjaindarshan.com/?p=1551)).

### Inferences
- **Indian venue tickets anchor at about ₹50–100.** The Kumbh DEC made about ₹50 per visitor; Mahakal, the wax museum and Akshardham charge ₹100. These are throughput businesses funded by capex. A content studio sells into them; it does not run them.
- **Rule 1 marks Dheevara apart from Indian practice.** Indian practice puts the deity at the centre: VR darshan of the mūrti, Ram Darbar holograms, a 3D Shiva at Isha, projection on the Virāṭ Svarūpa. Dheevara cannot sell "hologram of Ram". It can sell "stand where Hanumān's companions stood" or "be Sañjaya's listener". That is a differentiator, but it also means Dheevara is not a fit for darshan-type briefs.
- **The Ram Katha Museum criticism comes from both sides.** "Spectacle doesn't replace … proper research" (secular critic) and political-narrative concerns together create an opening for a *sourced* layer that institutions can point to as neutral. It also shows how quickly immersive mythology is read as political.
- **Kurukshetra's plan to rotate episodes** is a natural fit for a canon-backed library of short chapters, since a museum needs new content without new capex.

## 2. International precedents and economics

### Takeaway
Two economics coexist abroad:
- **Mega-capex hits** (ABBA Voyage ~£140M; Sphere's *Wizard of Oz*; teamLab Tokyo, 4.2M visits a year). These work on IP plus huge throughput at $35–120+ tickets.
- **A wave of failed projection pop-ups.** Lighthouse Immersive went bankrupt, Illuminarium Las Vegas closed, Meow Wolf had two rounds of layoffs.

Heritage-specific travelling formats (Eternal Notre-Dame, 675k visitors across 7+ cities) are the closest analogue to a touring "Mahābhārata witness" show, at a modest scale.

### Cited findings

**Tokyo, London and Paris venues**
- **teamLab (Tokyo).** 4.2M+ visitors in 2025 across its two Tokyo museums: Planets about 2.51M, Borderless about 1.69M (company ticket data, unaudited) ([teamLab press via EAP, Feb 2026](https://www.eap-magazin.de/Article/teamLab-Welcomes-More-Than-4.2-Million-Visitors-in-Tokyo.html)). Planets passed 10M cumulative visitors from 211 countries in Sep/Oct 2025, about half of them international ([teamLab](https://www.teamlab.art/press/borderless-planets2025/)).
- **Lightroom (London, King's Cross).** A joint venture of 59 Productions and the London Theatre Company, backed by investors led by Leonard Blavatnik. *David Hockney: Bigger & Closer* drew 75,000 visitors in its first month (2023) and was extended. It returned 17 Jun – 13 Oct 2024. Adult tickets were **£25** ([BroadwayWorld](https://www.broadwayworld.com/westend/article/Lightroom-Extends-DAVID-HOCKNEY-BIGGER-CLOSER-20230329); [Lonely Planet](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/news/david-hockney-exhibition-london); [Attractions Management](https://www.attractionsmanagement.com/Attractions-Management-magazine/Immersion-Lightroom-up-close-and-personal/36380)).
- **Frameless (London, Marble Arch).** 30,000 sq ft on a 15-year lease, opened Oct 2022, with a forecast of 750k visitors a year. Sponsorship is sold via CSM. No audited results were found ([Blooloop](https://blooloop.com/technology/news/frameless-immersive-art-experience-opening-london); [Sponsorship.org](https://sponsorship.org/csm-appointed-as-exclusive-sponsorship-sales-agency-for-londons-first-digital-immersive-arts-experience-frameless/)).
- **Culturespaces.**
  - Atelier des Lumières (Paris): about 1.2M visitors in 2018, nearly 1.4M in 2019.
  - Carrières de Lumières: about 700–770k a year.
  - Group total: about 4M a year. No revenue found ([Asharq Al-Awsat](https://english.aawsat.com/node/2396586); [Culturespaces press](https://presse.culturespaces.com/?p=2410)).

**North American projection venues**
- **Lighthouse Immersive** (Immersive Van Gogh) filed Chapter 15 in Delaware on **28 Jul 2023** with Ontario insolvency proceedings, after 7M+ tickets ([Blooloop, 4 Aug 2023](https://blooloop.com/technology/news/lighthouse-immersive-van-gogh-experience-bankruptcy/)). - **Meow Wolf.**
  - Spring 2024: cut 165 jobs (~10% of expenses) and closed its NYC office ([KOAA](https://www.koaa.com/news/covering-colorado/meow-wolf-cutting-165-positions-across-the-company-including-at-denver-location)).

  - It partnered with Niantic Spatial to extend its worlds into location AR ([Niantic Spatial](https://nianticspatial.com/blog/awe-usa-2025)).

**IP-led mega shows**
- **ABBA Voyage** (Aniara Ltd).
  - About **£140M** production cost (Bloomberg, 2022) ([Planet Attractions](https://www.planetattractions.com/news/‘Bloody-expensive’:-ABBA-need-to-recoup-£140m-to-cover-costs-of-virtual-Voyage-residency/1303)).

Per-show costs are reported as elevated ([News3LV](https://www.news3lv.com/news/local/the-wizard-of-oz-at-sphere-sells-over-500000-tickets-generating-65-million-in-sales); [TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/wizard-of-oz-at-sphere-makes-2-million-per-day/); [8 News Now](https://digital-release.8newsnow.com/news/local-news/the-wizard-of-oz-at-sphere-in-las-vegas-sells-more-than-1-5-million-tickets/amp/)).

**Festival and academic work**

  - HimmapanVR, a Thai mythical-creature museum: n=30, gains in knowledge and perceived authenticity ([Heliyon, May 2024](https://doaj.org/article/6ffc22dff78c45e68245ad67dc1a475e))

### Inferences
- **Break-even depends on IP plus throughput plus price, not technology.** ABBA needs about 1M visitors a year at roughly £100 of revenue each (my arithmetic: £104.3M ÷ 1.06M) and is still thin on profit. Projection pop-ups at $35 with generic public-domain art failed. Indian tickets are 1/10th to 1/30th of these prices, so a Dheevara-run venue would not pay back. Licensing content to funded venues would.
- **Eternal Notre-Dame is the best template** for a touring "witness" piece: a single heritage story, 45 minutes, free-roam, scholar-built (Seillier), a 7-city tour and about 675k visitors. In ticket terms that is very roughly €13–20M lifetime at €20–30 (my rough estimate, unverified).
- **The cautionary pattern is generic immersive without story.** The closures cite fading novelty; the survivors (teamLab, Sphere, ABBA) have strong authorship or IP. Dheevara's canon and the witness POV are the "authorship" element.

### Gaps
- **Not found:** Lightroom financials; the *Moving Bodies* show; Sphere's *Wizard of Oz* production cost; teamLab ticket prices and revenue; Frameless accounts (Companies House not checked).
- **Not found:** a Kingdom of Ayutthaya VR or Gilgamesh VR product; British Museum and Smithsonian AR specifics; any Tribeca myth or heritage winner.
- **Eternal Notre-Dame:** revenue and capex.

## 3. Holograms specifically

### Takeaway
In India, the "hologram" in practice means:
- **Proto boxes** for living gurus (Sadhguru, Mahashivratri 2026)
- **Large 3D or Pepper's-ghost-style deity visualisations** at festivals and museums (Shiva at Isha; the planned Ram Darbar in Ayodhya)
- **Posthumous concert holograms** (Sidhu Moose Wala, Toronto, Oct 2026).

No temple-sanctity controversy over holograms was found. The documented backlash is about AI *video* and games. Posthumous holograms draw "is this appropriate?" questions.

### Cited findings
- **Sadhguru AI hologram (Isha Yoga Centre, Coimbatore, Mahashivratri, Feb 2026).**
  - Proto units ran one-on-one sessions with an **AI avatar of Sadhguru** answering questions and guiding yoga in several languages, with downloadable selfies.

## 5. Audience response and sensitivity

### Takeaway
Devotees widely accept *darshan-substitutes* run by the temple itself:
- Kashi's VR (priests watched it and approved)
- TTD's queue VR
- Isha's holograms.

They react sharply when a third party commercialises or alters sacred imagery. 2025–26 examples include:
- the Roblox Tirumala probe
- Puri Jagannath AI-image complaints (97 images removed)
- Varanasi's eight FIRs
- a Doraemon-in-the-garbhagṛha AI video at Mahakal
- the Raipur "Jesus" AI-video FIR (repo).

The line falls at institution plus reverence (accepted) against outsider plus monetisation or alteration (backlash).

### Cited findings
- **Accepted:**
  - Kashi Vishwanath's VR darshan was screened to 363 temple employees and priests, who gave positive feedback, before the public trial ([DT Next](https://www.dtnext.in/news/national/up-kashi-vishwanath-temple-introduces-virtual-reality-darshan-for-devotees-790298)).

### Inferences
- **Dheevara is an outsider studio** and must borrow institutional legitimacy:

- **For kids, school uptake runs through IKS policy:** PRERANA, the IIT outreach and state teacher training. Short cited chapters plus a JS interactive suit these better than headset VR, which has age limits.

## 7. Positioning implications

### Takeaway
"Sourced + immersive witness experiences" sits in an empty cell:
- **Indian immersive work** is deity-centred spectacle or darshan, unsourced and sometimes read as political.
- **International immersive work** is authored art or IP spectacle.
- **Heritage VR** (Notre-Dame) is closest but not sacred-narrative.

Dheevara can extend the repo's "Stand beside the story" line to every surface: phone, glasses, dome or hologram. Its pitch to institutions is "the research layer for immersive mythology". The main risks:
- being lumped in with the AI-deity backlash
- being drawn into deity-centred briefs that break Rule 1
- venue economics
- glasses publishing being gated.

### Cited findings (anchors for the positioning)
- **Deity-centred Indian immersive work:** Ram Darbar hologram, Shiva 3D, VR darshan (§1, §3).
- **Expert critique of spectacle without research:** "Spectacle doesn't replace learning and proper research" ([The Art Newspaper](https://www.theartnewspaper.com/2025/01/22/museum-india-grand-ram-temple-ayodhya)).
- **Outsider monetisation of sacred space triggers probes:** TTD on Roblox ([Siasat](https://www.siasat.com/virtual-tirumala-temple-on-roblox-sparks-outrage-ttd-orders-probe-3239073/)).
- **Heritage touring VR works at modest scale:** 675k visitors ([Arsenal Contemporary](https://www.arsenalcontemporary.com/mtl/exhib/detail/eternalnotredame-2026)).
- **Generic immersive is in retreat:** the Illuminarium Las Vegas closure, "fifth major closure" ([Casino.org](https://www.casino.org/news/tourists-less-immersed-in-las-vegas-trend/)); the Lighthouse bankruptcy ([Blooloop](https://blooloop.com/technology/news/lighthouse-immersive-van-gogh-experience-bankruptcy/)).
- **Glasses reach in Hindi at ₹44,300,** with a web/JS developer path ([VR.org](https://vr.org/articles/ray-ban-meta-gen-3-india-44300-rupees-price-ladder-2026); [VR.org](https://vr.org/articles/meta-ray-ban-display-developer-sdk-2026)).

### Candidate positioning lines that include immersive (inferences; to test)
1. **"Stand beside the story — on your phone, in your glasses, in the temple courtyard."** This extends the existing brand line (repo) across surfaces; witness POV is the constant.
2. **"Every immersive epic, sourced."** This is the B2B and institutional line: Dheevara as the cited-canon and witness-POV studio behind museum, light-show and glasses experiences.
3. **"Hear it where it happened."** For glasses and audio at Kurukshetra, Ayodhya, Hampi and Laṅkā sites: a chanted verse, a witness's account, and the citation one tap away.
4. **"Not a darshan. A witness."** An internal or partner-facing line separating Dheevara from VR darshan, so temples see no substitute for worship. It is too blunt for consumer copy.
5. **"Lit by one lamp, checked against the verse."** For the hologram or relief-show format, joining the look to the proof.

### Risks (inferences)
1. **Category contamination.** "AI + deity + immersive" now carries FIRs (Puri, Varanasi, Raipur) and probes (Roblox Tirumala). Lead with sourcing and institutional partners. Disclosure stays per the repo rules.
2. **Brief creep against Rule 1.** Indian buyers (trusts, state boards) ask for deity holograms and darshan. Write a refusal clause into the B2B offer and propose a witness-POV alternative.
3. **Political capture.** Immersive mythology in Ayodhya is already read as a Hindutva narrative (The Art Newspaper). A state contract can tag Dheevara ideologically. Mitigation: scholar sign-off records (Rule 9) and "no judgement" reveal wording (repo).
4. **Venue economics.** Low Indian ticket prices and the global closures argue for licensing, not operating.
5. **Platform gating.** Meta toolkit publishing is limited to select partners, and Bose shows audio AR can be withdrawn. Keep glasses a channel on top of the phone app, not a platform bet.
6. **Rule 5 on glasses.** Glasses AI assistants send inputs to the platform. Unreleased canon must not pass through Meta AI.

# Scholar review: s1e3 mockups

**Verdict: not cleared. The core verse and its 7.34.19 citation are right. But s09 shows a verse that is not in the critical edition, and the chapter states popular details as fact (seven rings, sixteen), which is what our own reveal criticises.**

How I checked: against the BORI critical-edition (CE) e-text (sacred-texts `mbs`, mirrored in cltk) and Hellwig's DCS, which also numbers this verse 7.34.19. My confidence that the reference is correct is high. I have not seen the printed volume, so page numbers are unverified. Verse numbers marked "c." are my counts and need checking.

1. **[blocker] s09.** `एहि वत्स भिन्धि व्यूहं … संशितव्रताः ॥` is not CE text. The page also leaves out v.18, where Abhimanyu's speech actually begins, so `अभिमन्युरुवाच` is in the wrong place. Verses 20–21 are genuine. The e-text reads `yudhā` where the mockup has `युधां`; check against the printed volume. Replace the page with the genuine 7.34.17–21, numbering every verse:
   `धनंजयो हि नस्तात गर्हयेदेत्य संयुगात् । क्षिप्रमस्त्रं समादाय द्रोणानीकं विशातय ॥ १७ ॥`
   `अभिमन्युरुवाच । द्रोणस्य दृढमव्यग्रमनीकप्रवरं युधि । पितॄणां जयमाकाङ्क्षन्नवगाहे भिनद्मि च ॥ १८ ॥`
   If it can't be corrected, label the page "Illustrative".

2. **[blocker] s16, s14.** "Abhimanyu did not learn the chakravyūha in the womb." denies a belief outright; the text only gives a different account. "No one taught him the way out." is not in the verse at all, which says *notsahe vinirgantum*, "I cannot get out." For s16 use: "**The Mahābhārata tells it differently.** In the critical edition, Abhimanyu says his father taught him to break the formation, and that he cannot get out if trouble comes." For s14 use: "The text: his father taught him (7.34.19)."

3. **[blocker] Title, s05, s10 ("Breaks six rings"), s17, s18, and the narration "wheel of seven rings".** The CE never gives the wheel rings. 7.33 describes kings set around Duryodhana, with Droṇa and Jayadratha at the front. 7.32 says only that Abhimanyu broke it *anekadhā*, "in many places". Seven rings is a later image. Label it as the popular telling, with a source, or reword. For s10 use: "Breaks into the wheel and fights alone · 7.34–48".

4. **[major] s08, the reveal.** "There is no womb in the verse" is true but weak.
   - **Is the womb story absent?** I found no womb story in CE 7.32–51. In 1.213, Abhimanyu learns archery from Arjuna, after birth. I believe it is absent from Nīlakaṇṭha's vulgate too. Confidence: high for the CE's main text, moderate for the vulgate; I have not checked the CE's apparatus and appendices.
   - **Where does it come from?** Later regional retellings, kathā and folk theatre, popularised by 20th-century film and TV. My confidence about any earliest source is low, so name none until DHE-4.
   - **Wording.** "Vyāsa's text" is indefensible, because the CE is a reconstruction. Say "the critical edition".

   Two cautions. Aṣṭāvakra learns in the womb within the epic itself (3.132). And this story underpins *garbha-saṃskāra*, which many families practise. Never imply that womb-learning is absurd.

   Suggested tier 2: "Abhimanyu tells Yudhiṣṭhira his father taught him to break in, but he cannot get out (7.34.19). Elsewhere he learns arms from Arjuna as a boy (1.213). The critical edition does not tell the womb story." For tier 1, add the variant in which Kṛṣṇa interrupts.

5. **[major] "Sixteen" (s05, s10, s18).** The CE calls him *bāla*, *śiśu* and *aprāptayauvana* (7.32), and gives no age. As I recall, sixteen comes from the vulgate Ādi Parva: Soma lends his son Varcas for sixteen years. CE 1.61 keeps only the Varcas identification. Cite it as the popular telling, or say "a boy not yet grown".

6. **[major] s05: "a wheel no Pāṇḍava can break".** In 7.34.15, Abhimanyu, Arjuna, Kṛṣṇa or Pradyumna can break it. Use: "a wheel only Arjuna and a few others can break, and Arjuna is gone."

7. **[major] s05, chapter 1: "Droṇa promises Duryodhana a Pāṇḍava king".** That merges this vow with the day-12 vow to capture Yudhiṣṭhira. In 7.32 (c. v.13), Droṇa vows to fell one of the enemy's foremost great warriors. Use: "Droṇa vows to fell a great Pāṇḍava warrior".

8. **[major] s09 chips.** "Proofed · double-keyed" cannot sit beside text written for the mockup. "Public domain text" is risky: the Droṇaparvan volume dates from c.1958, and its status outside India is unclear. Until that is checked, use "BORI critical edition". Confidence: moderate.

9. **[major] s02 and s18: Telugu.** `తెలుగు` is spelled correctly but renders garbled: a bitmap fallback font with broken vowel signs. Tiro Telugu did not load.

10. **[minor] s06.** The Devanagari, sandhi, avagraha and daṇḍas are correct, and the verse scans as a *pathyā* anuṣṭubh.
    - Add `॥ १९ ॥` and close the IAST with `//`.
    - s06 splits `विनिर्गन्तुम् / अहं` while s09 joins it; choose one style.
    - The translation adds "find the way". Use: "My father taught me the method of breaking the array, but if any calamity befalls me I cannot come out."

11. **[minor] s07: "He does not know the way out."** Use "He cannot fight his way out."

12. **[minor] JS end card (frame on s17 shot 06): `यतो धर्मस्ततो जयः`.** It is genuine (e.g. 6.21, 11.17) but uncited. Over a boy killed against the rules of war it reads ironic. Use 7.48 (c. v.22): *eko 'yaṃ nihataḥ śete naiṣa dharmo mato hi naḥ*, "One against six he lies slain; this, we hold, was not dharma."

13. **[minor] The boon (claim `mbh-3.256-boon`, JS reveal).** 3.256 and its retelling at 7.41 are correct, but neither says "for one day"; remove those words. For the gate, cite 7.41–42 (7.42: the path he opened was blocked by Jayadratha).

14. **[minor] s04 and s11.**
    - "find the wheel broken" should be "find his son slain" (7.50).
    - "will not see another sunset" should be "to kill Jayadratha tomorrow" (7.51).
    - "No one followed him in" should be "They could not follow him in" (7.41).

15. **[minor] s10.**
    - "princess of Virāṭa" should be "daughter of King Virāṭa of Matsya" (4.67 is correct).
    - Āśvamedhika needs passage numbers: 14.65–69.
    - The CE spells the name Parikṣit; whichever spelling you use, keep it consistent.
    - The span 7.34–48 is correct: he is killed by Duḥśāsana's son in 7.48.

16. **[minor] Sensitivity.**
    - s17 "deity face": no deity should be on this field, since Kṛṣṇa is away with Arjuna.
    - Show the death by suggestion only.
    - s08 shows a sign-off dated 28 Sep that has not happened.
    - s13 `दी` is not a word.

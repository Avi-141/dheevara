How a chapter works
A page opens into a scene
Each chapter begins on the verse, chanted, then the page turns into the moment it describes. Two minutes.
You are a witness
You stand beside the event, with Sañjaya, a charioteer, a vānara in the army. Never inside a deity.

Works with the screen off
Every chapter is complete as audio. Lock your phone and keep listening.

The popular telling
Abhimanyu learned the way into the wheel while still in his mother’s womb, hearing Arjuna describe it to Subhadrā. She fell asleep before the way out.
Retold in film, television and family storytelling
What the text says
Abhimanyu tells Yudhiṣṭhira that his father taught him how to break in, and that he cannot get out. There is no womb in the verse.

गुरुWho taught him?
What many of us heard

Abhimanyu learned the way in while still in his mother’s womb, as Arjuna described it to Subhadrā. In some tellings she falls asleep; in others Kṛṣṇa interrupts before the way out.

Regional retellings, kathā and folk theatre, popularised by film and television
What the text says

Abhimanyu tells Yudhiṣṭhira his father taught him to break in, but that he cannot get out (7.34.19). Elsewhere he learns arms from Arjuna as a boy (1.213). The critical edition does not tell the womb story.
